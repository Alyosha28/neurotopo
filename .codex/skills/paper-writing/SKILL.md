# Paper writing

Use for manuscript work when the underlying results, deviations and four-way decisions are recorded and reproducible. Infrastructure logs or preliminary experiments can be documented without presenting them as publication-ready evidence. Do not choose preferred outcomes while writing.

Build a claim-to-evidence map from experiment records and primary sources. Check novelty against `research/prior_art/novelty_boundary.md`. Preserve numbers, citations, methods, negative results and uncertainty. Distinguish observations, estimates, mechanistic interpretations and speculation.

Organize connected paragraphs around the question, why the method identifies it, results, interpretation and the limits that change that interpretation. Use a reverse outline to give each paragraph a purpose; avoid a chronology of implementation work or a mandatory paragraph template.

**Mandatory anti-defensive-writing pass:** read [references/anti-defensive-writing.md](references/anti-defensive-writing.md). State supported findings directly, remove redundant self-defense and consolidate repeated caveats. Preserve material limitations and rival explanations. Confident prose must not raise the claim beyond evidence.

On revision, keep a reviewable source-to-revision diff; identify substantive deletions and validate that every numerical and causal claim still matches the evidence. Put necessary scope in the sentence/section where its removal would mislead. Never hide failed seeds or move an essential qualifier somewhere readers cannot find it.

References, pinned upstream commits, licenses and rejected advice: `docs/skill_reference_audit.md`. This is an original project adaptation, not an installation of any upstream skill wholesale.
