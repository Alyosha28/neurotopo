## Question and strongest rejection

At the pre-evidence query in the original full-noise task, is the correct value of the **three candidates not selected by the prior cue** less linearly decodable from an independent-block GRU's hidden state than from the degree/self-loop-matched random-mask GRU at delay32? EXP-0004 measured early output accuracy for the prior candidate, not direct access to all three alternatives.

Even if a linear ridge probe performs worse on the block model, it does not establish less total information, nor that candidate states correspond to semantic hypotheses. Different state geometry or nonlinear readout difficulty can cause the gap. The pretrained readout itself may rely on task-specific transformations after the late selector. No ablation here identifies a unique cause of EXP-0004's behavioral deficit.

## Probe, resources and checks

For each model×seed×delay fit a separate fixed-capacity linear ridge decoder from 64 state features plus intercept to sixteen one-hot targets (4 candidate IDs × 4 labels). Standardize each feature with **training-only** mean and population SD floored at 0.001, apply that transform unchanged to validation/test, use float64 normal equations, ridge λ=1 on the 64 slopes and no penalty on the intercept. Solve once; no hyperparameter sweep, stopping rule or checkpoint selection. Classification is independent argmax over each candidate's four logits, with **no permutation constraint**. Decoder has 1,040 coefficients for every model. Report train/validation/test fit counts, elapsed time, state bytes and normal-equation operations as a labelled proxy; full inference cost remains accounted separately.
