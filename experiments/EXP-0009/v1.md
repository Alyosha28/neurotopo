## One question and claim ceiling

Does a four-block independent GRU reduce **d=4 final joint-posterior KL** relative to a degree-, self-loop-, state-, effective-parameter- and dense-execution-matched random-mask GRU after the same varied-cadence training? Tested chain: recurrent connection organization → learned state dynamics → posterior computation/readout → nuisance-duration transfer. This is a scoped structural effect question. Even a positive result would not show candidate-specific hypotheses, attractor escape, SAE, biological value or general sequence-modeling superiority. Closest prior art and reuse decision: `research/prior_art/exp0009_scope.md` (RIMs, TRNN, conn2res and NeuroGym).

Strongest rejection: one fixed training schedule and generator may favor a graph's optimization or state mixing; changed block connectivity is not a semantic trajectory intervention. The d=4 transfer also combines longer recurrent evolution with more nuisance payload. A wide dense H64 GRU has more effective weights and is a **contextual practical baseline**, not an exactly matched causal control. The random graph is degree/self-loop matched but not claimed uniformly sampled over all such graphs. Record the number of edges crossing the original four block boundaries for each random graph; do not infer that modularity alone caused any difference.

## Metrics, uncertainty and decision

Primary seed-level effect `Δ = KL(C0,d4,final) − KL(M3,d4,final)`, positive favoring blocks. The confirmatory claim is **Δ>0.05 nat**, not merely a positive direction with a point estimate above0.05. Replication units are the ten paired model/graph/training seeds; one shared synthetic final stream is held fixed. Report each seed's KL, pre/final oracle-MAP agreement, H/R/joint true-label accuracy, initial/final parameter and mask/data hashes, runtime and resource accounts. Use deterministic20,000-resample paired-seed percentile bootstrap (seed694001) for mean Δ, SD and95%CI. Do not treat 4,096 episodes as independent model replications; the interval is conditional on this test stream. Secondary: d=1/8 outcomes, M1 contextual scores, learning curves and latency; none replace d=4.

Adequacy: for both M3 and C0, mean d=1 final KL≤0.05 and every seed≤0.10. Control headroom: C0 mean d=4 final KL in[0.07,1.00] nat and at least8/10 seeds≥0.05. Otherwise the structural transfer decision is **INCONCLUSIVE**, while practical learning failures or ceilings are still reported. A floor of M3 alone is an adverse result, not a reason to omit it.

Apply rules in order after adequacy/headroom/resource checks: `SUPPORTED` requires mean Δ≥0.05 **and lower95%CI>0.05**, M3−C0 mean d=1 KL≤0.01, and M3−C0 mean d=4 H true accuracy≥−0.01. `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` applies if lower95%CI>0 and either (a) the interval still includes0.05 (lower≤0.05≤upper) or (b) the interval excludes effects≤0.05 but an IID KL or H-accuracy cost guard fails; this supports a positive direction or a tradeoff, **not** a confirmed useful gain without cost. `FALSIFIED` for the registered >0.05-nat primary gain applies if upper95%CI<0.05, even if a smaller positive direction is possible. At a CI boundary equal to0 or0.05, the strict `>`/`<` conditions do not pass; remaining cases are `INCONCLUSIVE`. Contextual M1 scores do not enter the causal decision. Report separate practical ranking without treating active-parameter-unmatched M1 as the primary null.

Even an independently reviewed and clean-checkout reproduced `SUPPORTED` outcome **does not by itself pass Gate 1**: it isolates one block-versus-one-rewiring-generator effect on one synthetic transfer task. Discussing the broader Gate 1 claim would additionally require separately registered state/active-parameter/compute/training-budget-matched strong single-stream and RIM-style modular comparisons, at least one independent task/transfer replication, and candidate-specific interventions if the word “trajectory” implies hypotheses. A failed or uncertain outcome does not authorize adding adaptation, communication, DARC, biological topology or a language model to rescue it. Before any scientific run, a no-outcome preflight must archive exact initial parameter equality (mask excepted), all planned graph degrees/self-loops/strong connectivity and cross-block edges, schedule/data determinism, model resource accounts and a passing source-hashed validation suite. The auto-research ledger must reach `REGISTERED → VALIDATED`; source/config/checkpoint/data/analysis outputs are immutable and no planned seed is replaced.
