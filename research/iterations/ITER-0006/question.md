# ITER-0006 — Selected answer after a revised late cue

Earlier experiments established high linear accessibility of **candidate labels before** late evidence and a directional loss when a short-delay linear mapping is applied to longer-delay states. This iteration uses the same frozen recurrent checkpoints and the original full-noise task to ask whether the **final selected label** remains linearly accessible after the late selector, concentrating on revise trials where the selected candidate differs from the prior.

EXP-0008 v1 fits fixed H64→4 ridge probes to both confirm and revise siblings kept together in each split. One revise row per held-out pair defines the primary accuracy; a wide conventional GRU and a second independent fitted head are controls. The analysis cannot infer total memory, semantic competing trajectories, biological principles or causation of the pretrained readout's error.

Gate1 remains closed and no recurrent mechanism changes. EXP-0004/0006/0007 decisions remain immutable.
