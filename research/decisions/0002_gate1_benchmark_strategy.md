# Decision: stop low-dimensional Bayes-task escalation

2026-09-24. CAL-0001 and CAL-0002 were deliberately calibrated without any M3 outcomes. Both excluded a last-cue shortcut and had genuine uncertainty in the sampled latent truth. Both were nevertheless almost perfectly fit by a conventional dense H64 GRU in posterior KL. The successive change from four explicit-reliability hypothesis weights to eight hidden-source joint weights did not create a discriminating architecture task. Their complete records and limitations are in `research/negative_results/` and `artifacts/calibration/`.

The independent packet-only reflection of CAL-0002 (`research/reflections/CAL-0002/`) identified the same small sufficient-statistic shortcut, the terminal KL ceiling and the inability of a terminal fit alone to establish online source-reliability revision. The main-agent response is to **stop adding latent regimes, distractors or reliability pairs as a difficulty search**. This prevents repeated calibration from becoming indirect model-selection on a hoped-for architecture effect.

Before another Gate 1 architecture experiment, require a task-level argument for why its computation is not reducible to a few stationary counts, an oracle-relative headroom calibration of a strong single-state baseline, and counterfactual evidence-update checks. If the next scientific question is sample efficiency or transfer rather than final IID accuracy, register that as a separate claim with a predeclared compute trajectory and matched inputs; do not relabel either failed calibration as support. Independently, revise the architecture definition if the intended claim truly concerns hypothesis-specific trajectories: EXP-0004's all-input independent blocks plus shared readout did not instantiate candidate-specific routing or causal specialization. Any new mechanism still requires RIMs prior-art comparison and a fresh registered matched test; no adaptation, DARC, connectome or LM follows from these calibrations.

Status: Gate 1 remains NOT_PASSED. EXP-0004's scoped FALSIFIED result and EXP-0008's INCONCLUSIVE result remain unchanged. This decision revises research strategy, not historical thresholds.
