# CAL-0002: hidden source uncertainty also saturates

2026-09-24. **Engineering calibration; no multi-trajectory comparison.** Frozen exploratory plan: `research/design/gate1_belief_benchmark_calibration_v2.md`; complete results: `artifacts/calibration/gate1_source_belief_v1.json`. The new factor was an unobserved, fixed source regime. The model saw source IDs and cues but had to infer which source was more reliable while estimating four content hypotheses. Eight joint posterior weights are sufficient for the exact filter.

On the separate 8,192-episode calibration stream, the exact oracle's true joint `(H,R)` MAP accuracy was **81.57%**, H accuracy **92.27%**, R accuracy **83.90%**; H MAP changed after late evidence on **17.97%**. Last-cue H accuracy was **59.94%** and assuming source 0 was reliable gave **82.35%** H accuracy. These shortcuts are weaker than the oracle.

Yet after the frozen 1,500-update budget, the H64 dense GRU's final joint-posterior KL was **0.002268 / 0.001821 / 0.002032 nat** for seeds201–203, below the predeclared 0.01-nat saturation threshold in every seed. H true accuracies were 92.26% / 92.27% / 92.29%, effectively the oracle's 92.27%; joint true accuracies 82.07% / 81.75% / 81.67% were close to the oracle's 81.57% despite some near-tied joint MAP disagreements. No architecture headroom was established. CAL-0002 is **not qualified** for a formal Gate 1 comparison.

The strategy revision is substantive: hiding one binary context variable raised the sufficient state from four to eight posterior weights, still far below H64. Two consecutive calibrations show that noisy labels, multiple cues, late belief changes and an unknown source regime can coexist with a nearly Bayes-optimal single recurrent state. Do not search additional reliability pairs or hidden-variable counts until a task-level argument explains why the representation/computation should challenge a matched single-state model. The next scientific design must specify the minimal sufficient statistic, the simpler algorithmic solution, and a predeclared way to distinguish structural generalization from optimization or extra compute. Gate 1 remains NOT_PASSED; EXP-0004's bounded negative decision is unchanged.
