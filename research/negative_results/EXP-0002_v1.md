# EXP-0002 v1 — Delay-sensitive learnability

The longer-budget diagnostic is **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED** only for short-delay baseline feasibility. All delay0 seeds achieved perfect held-out joint success, but delay8 remained near chance in the early answer after 6,000 updates. Complete seed/checkpoint records are in `artifacts/EXP-0002/v1/`.

This rules out the assertion that the implementation cannot learn any generator condition. It does not rule out optimization, gradient propagation, nuisance interference, insufficient inductive bias, or a harder associative binding problem at delay8. Because multiple features distinguish this regime from a solved tiny fixed set, do not identify a single cause without another experiment.

No multi-trajectory comparison was run in this diagnostic, and no architectural stage gate advances. EXP-0001 v2 remains INCONCLUSIVE. Any revised training/task design requires a new registered protocol and fresh evaluation streams; existing outputs and thresholds are immutable.
