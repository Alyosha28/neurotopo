# What the frozen-state diagnostics establish

The generic multi-trajectory stage has a bounded result: under a curriculum that made the primary models learn the IID delayed-evidence task, the independent-block GRU did **worse** than a degree-, self-loop-, state-, parameter- and compute-matched random-mask GRU at the preregistered long-delay endpoint. In EXP-0004, delay32 joint success was 57.94% versus 67.04%; the paired difference was −9.10 percentage points (95% seed-bootstrap interval [−14.90,−3.60]). That falsifies its registered ≥3-point gain prediction for this architecture, task and training regime. It does not determine the cause or judge all modular systems.

Follow-up experiments separated several explanations without repairing that result:

| Diagnostic | Registered result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| [EXP-0005](../iterations/ITER-0003/decision.md): remove distractor payload at frozen weights | **INCONCLUSIVE**; both models lose accuracy, and the control violates the preregistered stability guard | Blank inputs create a large distribution shift; a reduced relative gap is not selective block rescue. |
| [EXP-0006](../iterations/ITER-0004/decision.md): decode uncued candidate labels before late evidence | **FALSIFIED** for a ≥5-point linear-access deficit; block 98.82%, control 99.30%, difference interval [−1.19,+0.14] pp | A large loss of linearly accessible uncued labels at this timepoint does not explain the task gap under this probe. Near-ceiling scores limit finer comparisons. |
| [EXP-0007](../iterations/ITER-0005/decision.md): apply a short-delay prior-answer ridge head at delay32 | **PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED** for a positive direction; block excess transfer loss +8.91 pp, interval [+1.57,+15.78] pp | The fixed ridge mapping transfers less well for the block model on average. The interval does not confirm the registered minimum +5-point effect or identify why the pretrained head fails. |
| [EXP-0008](../iterations/ITER-0006/decision.md): decode final selected answer on revise trials | **INCONCLUSIVE** for a ≥3-point deficit; new-head block/control 94.33%/95.76%, difference interval [−3.30,+0.31] pp | Newly supervised linear heads read the revised answer from both frozen states at high absolute accuracy. Their model contrast and the cause of pretrained-head errors remain uncertain. |

Together, these results make a simple “the block model forgot the alternative candidate values” account inadequate **for the measured linear probe family**. They distinguish information accessible to a newly fitted readout at a tested delay from what the originally trained shared head computes after untrained delay steps. Cross-delay probes give directional evidence of reduced mapping transfer in the block model, while final-state probes leave a modest between-model difference unresolved. None of the observational probes identifies a causal mediator: longer sequences accumulate distractor input as well as recurrent updates, probe objectives differ from the original head, and all intervals reuse ten trained model/graph seeds and one fixed synthetic test stream per experiment.

Gate1 is **not passed**. The program has not shown competing semantic hypotheses, attractor escape, SAE, biological topology benefit or general sequence-modeling superiority. The registered STOP_MECHANISM_ESCALATION branch remains in force; adding adaptation, communication, connectome wiring or an LM would not follow from these results. Full protocols, manifests, seeds, independent reviews and clean-checkout subset reproductions are preserved under `experiments/`, `artifacts/` and `research/iterations/`.
