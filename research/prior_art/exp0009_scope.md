# EXP-0009 closest prior art and reuse decision

2026-09-24. The proposed comparison concerns **fixed block versus degree/self-loop-matched rewired recurrence** on posterior maintenance after unseen nuisance duration. RIMs already uses multiple recurrent modules with selective routing and communication; the proposed all-active independent-block GRU is a deliberately simpler structural baseline and cannot claim RIM-style hypotheses or module specialization. TRNN already studies sparse/hierarchical recurrence and transient dynamics on delayed working-memory tasks; no adaptation/transient mechanism is introduced here. conn2res already studies network architecture × recurrent dynamics and computational capacity, though its frozen reservoirs/readouts differ from the present BPTT-trained GRUs. See the audited repositories, licenses, implementations and limitations in `evidence_matrix.md`, `mandatory_baselines.md` and `reusable_projects.md`.

[NeuroGym ProbabilisticReasoning](https://neurogym.github.io/envs/ProbabilisticReasoning-v0.html) already implements sequential likelihood integration, and [ContextDecisionMaking](https://neurogym.github.io/envs/ContextDecisionMaking-v0.html) covers contextual decisions. CAL-0001/0002 established that NeuroTopo's small known-oracle variants are not novel task families and can be saturated by a dense GRU. CAL-0003/0004 isolated a new **training-condition qualification**, not a literature novelty claim: varied nuisance-count training with equal total tokens rescued a fixed-cadence d=4 floor. EXP-0009 would test only whether the recurrent **connection organization** then changes transfer under matched resources.

Do not import RIMs/TRNN source: current code review found license/test and architectural-assumption mismatches, and the requested intervention uses the already validated NeuroTopo GRU mask. Do not add NeuroGym as a dependency for an exact eight-class posterior and paired counterfactual generator already owned and tested locally. External software choice does not set the scientific claim. A positive block-versus-random result would require independent reproduction and cannot establish semantic candidate trajectories, attractor escape, biological topology or general sequence-modeling superiority.
