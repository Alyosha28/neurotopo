# Evidence-integration task overlap for Gate 1 calibration

Checked 2026-09-24 against [NeuroGym's ProbabilisticReasoning documentation](https://neurogym.github.io/envs/ProbabilisticReasoning-v0.html) and [its published source](https://neurogym.github.io/_modules/neurogym/envs/probabilisticreasoning.html). This environment presents a sequence of stimuli whose log-likelihood contributions add to a two-choice decision, separated by delays. The source computes the final log odds by summing shape weights. [PerceptualDecisionMaking](https://neurogym.github.io/envs/PerceptualDecisionMaking-v0.html) and [PulseDecisionMaking](https://neurogym.github.io/envs/PulseDecisionMaking-v0.html) are further evidence-accumulation tasks. Sequential likelihood integration is therefore established task design, not a NeuroTopo novelty claim.

NeuroTopo's immediate calibration needs four explicitly tracked latent hypotheses, two supervised belief readouts separated by late evidence, known cue reliabilities, exact posterior targets and tagged distractors. The inspected NeuroGym `ProbabilisticReasoning` task exposes a two-alternative final decision and trial-environment interface; adapting it would still require a different generator and oracle for these controls. A small owned generator is justified for this bounded calibration. No NeuroGym code is copied, installed or treated as evidence that the proposed multi-trajectory architecture helps.

Relevant architecture overlap remains RIMs (multiple recurrent modules), TRNN (transient working-memory dynamics) and conn2res (network × dynamics computational capacity), as recorded in `evidence_matrix.md`. This calibration tests task discriminability before another architecture comparison; it does not claim semantic trajectory specialization.

Checked [NeuroGym ContextDecisionMaking](https://neurogym.github.io/envs/ContextDecisionMaking-v0.html) and [HierarchicalReasoning](https://neurogym.github.io/envs/HierarchicalReasoning-v0.html) for the next calibration: the former gives an explicit rule cue selecting a relevant modality, while the latter has unannounced block transitions. These are adjacent context-inference/decision paradigms. NeuroTopo's proposed hidden-source reliability task is a controlled synthetic joint-posterior diagnostic, not an assertion that hidden context or rule switching is novel.
