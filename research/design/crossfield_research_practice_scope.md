# Cross-field research-practice audit scope (2026-09-28)

Question: In dynamical systems neuroscience, predictive-state/control theory, and causal intervention or error-correction research, how do researchers turn a recurrent state phenomenon into a falsifiable mechanism study, and which methods transfer to NeuroTopo EXP0025?

Evidence scope: Primary papers and author/official paper pages, preferably the methods and experimental designs. Inspect at least one source per chosen field; keep source claims separate from our methodological inference. Existing EXP0025 outcomes define the local case, not external proof. Search aliases for same-current-output/different-future, state-space dynamics, predictive states, and recurrent-state correction. Record sources, date, overlap, limits and a concrete decision map.

Strongest rejection: A state intervention that preserves an immediate logit can still alter many hidden variables; a successful case may be a supervised two-state codebook. A field's elegant explanation may not transfer to this model or establish novelty. The report must not infer a unique causal decomposition, stable group effect, publication acceptance, or Gate1 evidence.

Decision rule: Accept a lesson only if a primary source states or demonstrates the associated method; label any transfer to NeuroTopo an inference and attach a falsifiable comparison using the frozen R/M results as context. If a paper is inaccessible, do not claim its detailed result. The task ends with a source-backed explanation of the research reasoning process and a bounded design matrix; it does not register or execute a new EXP or alter existing thresholds.
